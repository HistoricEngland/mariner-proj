What is the Marine Historic Environment Record Service for England?

The Marine HER Service for England is the first port of call for anyone seeking information about heritage that is located in England’s seas and tidal waters, from the mean high water mark out to the edge of the offshore marine planning area. We provide access to, and maintenance and enhancement of, the National Marine Heritage Record.

What is the National Marine Heritage Record?

The National Marine Heritage Record (NMHR) documents England’s coastal, intertidal and underwater heritage, from the prehistoric submerged landscapes of Doggerland in the North Sea, to WWII sea defences, to newly discovered wrecks. It is constantly updated with the latest available research, by Historic England marine and coastal specialists.

What type of records do we hold?

We hold records detailing archaeological investigations of coastal structures, and intertidal and underwater sites including over 6,000 shipwrecks and aircraft wrecks, obstructions, submerged prehistoric landscapes, marine buildings and structures (such as seaforts, fish traps, docks, harbours, jetties, sea defences), and find spots. These records are based on archaeological reports, radio carbon dates, reports from Marine aggregate extractions, UKHO data, historic seascape characterisation, local and project data sets (including Citizan), recreational diver information, and Receiver of Wreck droit finds. We also hold records that document 31, 000 recorded vessel losses, where no physical site has been found, based on historical documentary evidence such as historic news articles and Lloyds listings.

What type of records do we provide?

Dependant on the type of the query, we can provide exports of our records, including the following in HTML format:

  • Wreck Sites
  • Site type
  • Maritime Vessels
  • Aircraft Crash Sites
  • Historic Aircraft
  • Monuments
  • Artefacts
  • Activities

All of our records can also be exported in CSV format, and where possible, geospatially in Shapefile format. Additional data and contextual information derived from GIS layers held by the Marine HER Service for England can be provided where relevant.

Where source materials cited in these records have been digitally archived by an external trusted repository, links through to these resources have been provided. Source materials held by the Marine HER Service for England will be viewable upon request. Please note that requests to view material held by the Historic England Archive should be directed to Archive Services.

Historic England is committed to the open sharing of data relating to nationally designated sites on the National Heritage List for England (NHLE), as well as other key datasets, through their Open Data Hub under the Open Government Licence v3 (OGL). Therefore, records in the NMHR that are designated as Protected Wreck sites and Scheduled Monuments are also present within the freely available NHLE datasets. However, the contents of these two datasets are not identical, with the NMHR more frequently updated with the latest research and fieldwork results. As such, we would recommend using both datasets.
